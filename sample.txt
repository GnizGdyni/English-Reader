The old library was quiet in the late afternoon. Sunlight filtered through tall windows and fell across wooden tables scattered with open books.

Alice paused at a familiar sentence: curiosity often leads to unexpected discoveries. She underlined the phrase with her finger and wondered about its meaning.

Nearby, a student whispered that perseverance matters more than talent. The librarian smiled, knowing that similar words — grit, patience, resolve — all pointed toward the same idea.

Outside, pigeons fluttered past the glass. Inside, the soft rustle of turning pages continued without interruption.
